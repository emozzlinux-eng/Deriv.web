# AI ANALYST

AI receives structured market data.

It may:

- Summarize market conditions
- Explain indicators
- Explain signals
- Identify conflicting evidence
- Explain risk
- Identify what to monitor

AI must NOT:

- Execute trades directly
- Override risk engine
- Modify security controls
- Invent prices
- Invent signals
- Claim certainty

Architecture:

```text
Market Data
     ↓
Analysis
     ↓
Structured Context
     ↓
AI Analyst
     ↓
Explanation
```

NOT:

```text
AI
 ↓
Deriv
```

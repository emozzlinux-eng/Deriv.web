# MULTI-TIMEFRAME ENGINE

Analyze multiple timeframes simultaneously.

Example:

```text
1H  → Macro trend
15M → Structure
5M  → Entry
```

Create:

```text
MTF MATRIX

TIMEFRAME | TREND | MOMENTUM | VOLATILITY | SETUP
---------------------------------------------------
1M        | ↑     | Strong   | High       | +
5M        | ↑     | Medium   | Medium     | +
15M       | ↑     | Strong   | Medium     | ++
1H        | →     | Weak     | Low        | 0
4H        | ↓     | Medium   | Medium     | -
```
